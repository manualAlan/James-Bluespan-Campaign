01 / The 2070 priorities

Lasting prosperity

Chasmia’s resource wealth should create lasting public capacity. Protect the wealth fund, invest it professionally, and keep it working for future generations. Pair that long-term approach with a welcoming environment for businesses and investment.

The commitments

  • Save resource wealth and safeguard the wealth fund.
  • Reward long-term investment and lower business taxes.
  • Give new firms and residents room to grow.
02 / The 2070 priorities

Education & families

Opportunity begins in the classroom and at home. Keep education at the heart of public investment, strengthen schools, support access to tertiary education, and build on the childcare and school-lunch programs that help families every day.

The commitments

  • Invest in schools and tertiary education grants.
  • Build on the refundable childcare boost for working families.
  • Support fully funded lunches for public and charter students.
03 / The 2070 priorities

Reliable, clean power

A growing Chasmia needs dependable power. The Verasul energy agenda brings modern AP1000 nuclear technology together with hydroelectric power to provide a reliable foundation for homes, industry, and future growth.

The commitments

  • Back modern nuclear power in Verasul.
  • Combine nuclear and hydro for dependable electricity.
  • Build the energy capacity that homes and industry need.
04 / The 2070 priorities

A connected Chasmia

Prosperity should reach every part of Chasmia. Invest in city infrastructure and rural roads, broadband, water, and agriculture. Build on ChasAir’s publicly backed, commercially managed model to connect people and businesses to opportunity.

The commitments

  • Invest in city infrastructure, rural roads, and broadband.
  • Strengthen water systems and agriculture.
  • Support regional links, passenger travel, cargo, and skilled jobs through ChasAir.

Invest today. Build for tomorrow.

Turn finite resource wealth into permanent public capacity. Protect the fund, invest in people and places, and make room for businesses to build the next generation of Chasmian prosperity.
